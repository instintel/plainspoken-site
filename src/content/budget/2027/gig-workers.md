*Updated Oct 11, 2026*

If you drive e-hailing or ride for deliveries, Budget 2027 brings a RM160 million package paid for together with Grab from 2027. It also tops up your EPF savings by up to RM600 a year, and the government matches part of your PERKESO contributions. If your side hustle is set up as a small company, its tax rate drops by one point too.

## The Grab package: up to RM227 a month more for drivers, RM100 for riders.

The package raises the minimum rate drivers and riders earn. It also helps with vehicle maintenance and insurance, plus PERKESO contributions. The government says it reaches about 600,000 e-hailing and delivery workers, adding up to RM227 a month to what an e-hailing driver takes home and up to RM100 for a delivery rider.

![The Grab package: up to RM227 a month more for drivers, RM100 for riders (Chart)](/images/budget/2027/chart-gig-income-2027-2026-10-11.png)

## EPF: the government adds up to RM600 a year to your retirement savings.

The Employees Provident Fund (EPF) is the retirement savings account most workers with a boss pay into from their salary. Gig workers don't have a boss doing that, so they save through i-Saraan, EPF's scheme for people who put money in themselves. When e-hailing and delivery workers pay into i-Saraan, the government matches it, up to RM600 a year and RM6,000 over a lifetime.

## PERKESO: the government matches 35% of your contribution, or 50% if the platform pays too.

PERKESO, also called SOCSO, is the social security scheme that pays out if you get hurt while working. For e-hailing and delivery workers, the government matches 35% of what you contribute, rising to 50% if the platform company contributes too. For over 200,000 self-employed people in 17 sectors where PERKESO isn't compulsory, the match is 70%.

## RM270 million in loans to start a business or buy a first home.

BSN and Agrobank, two government-owned banks, get RM270 million to lend to gig workers, either to start a business or to buy a first home.

## If your side hustle is a company, its tax drops one point.

From the 2027 year of assessment, small and medium companies pay 14% instead of 15% on their first RM150,000 of taxable profit. On the part from RM150,001 to RM600,000, they pay 16% instead of 17%. The government says about 300,000 of these businesses could save up to RM6,000 a year.

## What happens next

- 2027: the Grab package starts, with no exact date given yet
- 2027 year of assessment: the lower company tax rates apply
