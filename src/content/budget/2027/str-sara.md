*Updated Oct 11, 2026*

Budget 2027 sets aside RM16 billion, up from RM15 billion this year, for two kinds of government help. STR is cash paid to lower-income households. SARA is credit put on your MyKad that you spend on basic goods at approved shops. If you're on STR, you get up to RM150 a month in SARA. If you're 18 or older and not on STR, you get RM100 of SARA twice in 2027.

## On STR: up to RM150 a month in SARA credit.

That's up to RM1,800 over the year, on top of the STR cash itself. The government expects up to nine million STR recipients to get it.

## Not on STR: RM100 twice, if you're 18 or older.

This part is called SARA MADANI, and it's for adult Malaysians who don't get STR, including middle-income households. About 13 million people should get it. The Ministry of Finance (MOF) expects the payments in February and August 2027, one before Hari Raya Aidilfitri and one before the 70th Merdeka.

So if you've just started working and earn too much for STR, this is the part that reaches you: RM200 over the year, spent at the shops on basic goods.

## You can now spend SARA at farmers' markets too.

SARA credit already works at participating shops. From 2027 it also covers fresh produce at 216 farmers' and tamu markets run with FAMA, the government agency that sells farm produce.

## Discount sales get more money as well.

Jualan Rahmah MADANI and Jualan Agro MADANI, the government's pop-up sales of cheaper groceries, go from RM630 million to RM750 million, with about 35,000 sales planned. Agro MADANI items sell for up to 30% below the usual price.

## What happens next

- February 2027: the first RM100 SARA MADANI, before Hari Raya Aidilfitri
- August 2027: the second RM100, before the 70th Merdeka
