*3 min read*

If you earn RM1,700 a month, the lowest pay the law allows, Budget 2027 lifts that floor by RM300, or about 18%, to RM2,000 from June 2027. Whether your payslip actually changes depends on how big your employer is.

![Photo of a worker welding on a factory floor.](/images/daily-briefs/2026-10-10/photo-minimum-wage-2026-10-10.jpg)

Micro, small and medium businesses (MSMEs) with yearly sales below RM50 million are exempt, which the government says gives them room to adjust how they run. It gave no end date for that. Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim announced the new minimum wage on Friday in Budget 2027, the government's spending plan for next year. The decision lands with about 3% of people who want a job still looking for one (July) and shop prices rising 1.9% a year (August), both the latest official readings.

So in June 2027, every worker sits in one of three places:

- Paid under RM2,000 at a firm selling RM50 million a year or more: your floor goes up to RM2,000.
- Paid under RM2,000 at a smaller firm: exempt for now, with no date for when that ends.
- Already on RM2,000 or more: this rule doesn't change your pay.

That second group is the open question. With no end date, workers at smaller firms have no date for when the RM2,000 floor reaches them.

For fresh grads, the bigger number in the speech was RM2,500. The government says it will reform how workers' pay is set, starting with a RM2,500 monthly minimum for graduates and semi-skilled workers. It gave no start date, so for now it doesn't change any offer letter.

Until June, RM1,700 is still the floor.

## Also in the Budget

**The government expects to spend about RM40 billion next year to keep fuel cheaper at the pump.**

The speech says fuel subsidies, the money the government puts in so you pay less, stay high in 2027. That works out to about half of everything set aside for subsidies, aid and incentives. It's the money behind the subsidised BUDI95 price you pay at the petrol pump.

**A bigger tax deduction for every resident taxpayer means less of your income gets taxed.**

Individual relief, the amount you can take off your income before the tax bill is calculated, goes up to RM12,000. It hasn't changed since 2010. The tax rate also drops by one percentage point for some middle-income earners, so more of your income is shielded from tax even if your rate stays the same.

**The government plans to borrow less, as a share of the economy, next year.**

Budget 2027 targets a deficit, the gap between what the government spends and what it collects, of 3.3% of everything Malaysia produces in a year, smaller than this year's estimate. The ringgit got slightly stronger on Friday, and a Bank Muamalat economist tied that to the smaller target. A stronger ringgit makes anything you pay in US dollars a little cheaper.

## What to watch

- **June 2027:** the RM2,000 minimum wage starts at firms that aren't exempt.
- **Thursday, Oct 15:** the next weekly fuel prices take effect.
- **Monday, Oct 19:** Malaysia's September trade figures, what we sold to other countries that month.
