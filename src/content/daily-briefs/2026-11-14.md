*3-minute read*

Every trading day since Thursday, Nov 5, has ended lower for [the KLCI](/explainers/klci/), Malaysia's main share index, and Friday's fifth fall in a row took it to 1,582.59, which local business paper The Edge called a 14-month low. The KLCI tracks the 30 biggest companies on Bursa Malaysia, so if you own Malaysian shares, it's the number that tells you how the big ones did, and this week it says down 2.1%.

Friday's own drop was small, just 3.37 points. The index ended last Friday at 1,616.58, and it was at 1,635.86 on Nov 5, the day Bank Negara Malaysia (BNM) kept [its main interest rate](/explainers/opr/) at 2.75%. It has gone down every session since.

![The KLCI fell five sessions in a row to 1,582.59 (Chart). Malaysia's main share index, the FBM KLCI, at each day's close since Oct 27, in index points. The shading marks five falls in a row from Nov 6.](/images/daily-briefs/2026-11-14/chart-klci-daily-close-2026-11-14.png)

This time technology shares were among the biggest losers, while the big bank shares that dragged the index down on Wednesday edged up on Friday. Thong Pak Leng, an analyst at Rakuten Trade, said investors stayed nervous about the money going into AI, and unsure about the change at the top of Nvidia, the biggest maker of AI chips. The Edge said investors kept selling shares linked to data centres.

That change happened on Thursday, when Nvidia's boss Jensen Huang quit after reports about his private life. The value of Nvidia's shares fell by just under US$600 billion this week, AP reported, and finance chief Colette Kress is running it for now.

So the next test of those AI worries is Nvidia's own results, out early on Wednesday, Nov 18, Malaysia time.

## Meanwhile, elsewhere

**Prices US businesses charge rose 6.5% in a year to October, the most since November 2022.**

The rise in October alone was a bit smaller than economists expected, and it's the last price reading before [the Federal Reserve](/explainers/federal-reserve/), the US central bank, decides on rates on Dec 10, Malaysia time. Bets on a December rise kept lifting the US dollar against the ringgit on Friday, said Bank Muamalat economist Mohd Afzanizam Abdul Rashid.

**Sales at Malaysia's shops, car dealers and wholesalers rose 15.9% in September from a year earlier.**

That's faster than August, with all three growing and wholesale growing fastest, the Department of Statistics Malaysia (DOSM) said. The amount of goods sold grew far less, so much of the rise came from higher prices. It lands just before Wednesday's full figure for how fast the economy grew from July to September.

**Wages paid by Malaysia's service businesses rose 4.6% from July to September, compared with a year earlier.**

Services, from shops and restaurants to transport and private hospitals, employ millions of Malaysians. The sector's revenue grew even faster than pay, which DOSM put down to "stronger consumer spending, tourism-related activities and demand for digital services".

**Malaysia's trade ministry says it never cleared the local arm of a company caught up in the Nvidia scandal.**

The Ministry of Investment, Trade and Industry (MITI) is now checking with Customs whether AI chips that the local arm of Megaspeed, a Singapore cloud computing company, brought into Malaysian data centres were sent on to other countries. Sending top US AI chips out of or through Malaysia has needed a MITI permit since July 2025.
