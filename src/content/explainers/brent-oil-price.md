*Updated Oct 21, 2026*

Brent is the oil price you hear about in the news. It's the price of a barrel of crude oil from the North Sea, quoted in US dollars, and the world uses it as a yardstick for other oil. On Oct 20, 2026, it rose above US$111 a barrel, its highest since May, before ending the day at about US$109.

Crude oil is the raw material. It isn't what you pay at the pump.

## Brent is named after one North Sea oil field, but it now covers several.

The name comes from the Brent field in the North Sea, between the UK and Norway, where production began in 1976. Output from that field has fallen, so the price now follows a basket of crude grades: Brent, Forties, Oseberg, Ekofisk and Troll from the North Sea. US WTI Midland crude was added from June 2023.

A yardstick, or benchmark, is a price other prices get compared with. The US Energy Information Administration (EIA) says Brent prices "often serve as the basis of comparison" for oil prices around the world.

## You'll see two Brent prices, one for cargoes and one for futures.

Dated Brent is an assessment of what physical cargoes of North Sea crude cost for loading on set dates. The price in most news reports is the other one: the Brent futures contract, traded on the ICE exchange in London. A futures contract is a deal to buy oil at a price agreed today, for delivery later.

## Brent is the crude price, and a weekly formula sets the pump price.

The Ministry of Finance (MOF) resets [weekly pump prices](/explainers/ron95-price/) with a formula. It "takes into account the average price of the previous week." Petrol and diesel are made from crude, so the two are linked. When MOF explained a weekly rise in May 2026, it pointed to supply constraints for both crude oil and petroleum products, caused by the conflict in West Asia.

## What it means for your money.

If you're on [BUDI95](/explainers/budi95/) and stay within your limit, you pay RM1.99 a litre whatever Brent does. A higher full price lands on the government's share instead.

You feel it more if you go past your limit, fill up RON97, or buy diesel for a business that pays the full price. Brent is priced in US dollars, so the [ringgit](/explainers/ringgit/) matters too: the weaker the ringgit, the more ringgit the same barrel costs.

## Brent was about US$109 on Oct 20, 2026.

This is the reading at that date. Oil prices change every day.

- Brent rose above US$111 after Yemen's Houthis claimed attacks on Saudi energy sites. Saudi authorities had not confirmed the claims.
- It fell back after an unconfirmed report of a proposal to revive US-Iran talks, and ended the day at US$108.79.
