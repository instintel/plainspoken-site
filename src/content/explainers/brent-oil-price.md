*Updated Nov 23, 2026*

Brent, the oil price you hear about in the news, is what a barrel of North Sea crude oil costs in US dollars, and it reaches you through the full price at the petrol pump. On Friday, Nov 20, 2026, it ended the day at US$92.57 a barrel, down from US$95.84 a week earlier, on Friday, Nov 13.

Crude oil is the raw material. It isn't what you pay at the pump. Petrol and diesel are made from crude, so the two are linked. The Ministry of Finance (MOF) cut the full RON95 price by 15 sen to RM3.72 a litre for Nov 19 to 25, 2026. It said prices of refined fuel in the region fell over the period its pricing formula counts, in line with lower world crude prices.

## Brent Is Named After One North Sea Field, And Has Two Prices.

The name comes from the Brent field in the North Sea, between the UK and Norway, where production began in 1976. Output from that field has fallen, so the price now follows a basket of crude grades: Brent, Forties, Oseberg, Ekofisk and Troll from the North Sea. US West Texas Intermediate (WTI) Midland crude was added from June 2023.

The Brent price is also a yardstick, or benchmark, meaning a price other prices get compared with. The US Energy Information Administration (EIA) says Brent prices "often serve as the basis of comparison" for oil prices around the world.

Dated Brent is an assessment of what physical cargoes of North Sea crude cost for loading on set dates. The price in most news reports is the other one: the Brent futures contract, traded in London on the Intercontinental Exchange (ICE). A futures contract is a deal to buy oil at a price agreed today, for delivery later.

## A Weekly Formula Sets The Pump Price From Regional Fuel Prices.

MOF resets [weekly pump prices](/explainers/ron95-price/) with a formula. It uses the average price over the previous week, MOF says.

On Nov 18, MOF also said world oil prices are expected to keep swinging in the near term. It said uncertainty in world politics and the risk of oil shipments being disrupted could still affect supply.

## Brent Reaches You Through The Full Pump Price And The Ringgit.

If you're on [BUDI95](/explainers/budi95/) and stay within your limit, your pump price doesn't move with Brent each week. A higher full price lands on the government's share instead.

You feel it more if you go past your limit, fill up RON97, or buy diesel for a business that pays the full price. Brent is priced in US dollars, so the [ringgit](/explainers/ringgit/) matters too: the weaker the ringgit, the more ringgit the same barrel costs.

## Brent Ended At US$92.57 On Nov 20, 2026.

This is the reading at that date, for the futures contract that delivers in January 2027.

- On Monday, Nov 16, Brent fell US$3.40 to US$92.44. Reuters said signs of more oil getting through the Strait of Hormuz added to expectations of a surplus, meaning more oil on offer than buyers need. The strait is the narrow sea lane Middle East oil ships sail through.
- On Friday, Nov 20, it rose 59 cents to US$92.57, but was still down 3.4% on the week, its third weekly drop in a row. Bloomberg said traders weighed the risk of new US strikes on Iran against plentiful crude supplies.

For a longer view, the EIA tracks Brent's spot price, the price for oil bought for immediate delivery. Its monthly average was US$123.93 a barrel in October 2026, above April's US$117.29 and well above the US$69.14 average for all of 2025.
