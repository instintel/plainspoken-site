*Updated Nov 3, 2026*

Brent is the oil price you hear about in the news. It's what a barrel of North Sea crude oil costs in US dollars, and the yardstick the world uses for other oil. On Monday, Nov 2, 2026, it ended the day at US$104.77 a barrel, down 7.6% from US$113.43 on Friday, Oct 30.

Crude oil is the raw material. It isn't what you pay at the pump. Petrol and diesel are made from crude, so the two are linked. The Ministry of Finance (MOF) raised the full RON95 price by 10 sen to RM4.42 a litre for Oct 29 to Nov 4, 2026. It said Brent rose the week before on concerns that supply from West Asia could be disrupted.

## Brent Is Named After One North Sea Field, And Has Two Prices.

The name comes from the Brent field in the North Sea, between the UK and Norway, where production began in 1976. Output from that field has fallen, so the price now follows a basket of crude grades: Brent, Forties, Oseberg, Ekofisk and Troll from the North Sea. US West Texas Intermediate (WTI) Midland crude was added from June 2023.

A yardstick, or benchmark, is a price other prices get compared with. The US Energy Information Administration (EIA) says Brent prices "often serve as the basis of comparison" for oil prices around the world.

Dated Brent is an assessment of what physical cargoes of North Sea crude cost for loading on set dates. The price in most news reports is the other one: the Brent futures contract, traded in London on the Intercontinental Exchange (ICE). A futures contract is a deal to buy oil at a price agreed today, for delivery later.

## Brent Is The Crude Price, And A Weekly Formula Sets The Pump Price.

MOF resets [weekly pump prices](/explainers/ron95-price/) with a formula. It "takes into account the average price of the previous week."

## Brent Reaches You Through The Full Pump Price And The Ringgit.

If you're on [BUDI95](/explainers/budi95/) and stay within your limit, your pump price stays the same whatever Brent does. A higher full price lands on the government's share instead.

You feel it more if you go past your limit, fill up RON97, or buy diesel for a business that pays the full price. Brent is priced in US dollars, so the [ringgit](/explainers/ringgit/) matters too: the weaker the ringgit, the more ringgit the same barrel costs.

## Brent Ended At US$104.77 On Nov 2, 2026.

This is the reading at that date.

- Brent ended October at its highest in six months, Reuters reported.
- On Monday it fell US$8.66. Reuters reported the drop came after Iran's president, Masoud Pezeshkian, said Iran's supreme leader had given the government some authority for peace talks.
- The US and Iran had not agreed a deal by then, Bloomberg reported.

For a longer view, the EIA tracks Brent's spot price, the price for oil bought for immediate delivery. Its monthly average was US$114.16 a barrel in September 2026, close to April's US$117.29 and well above the US$69.14 average for all of 2025.
