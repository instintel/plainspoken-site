*Updated Nov 23, 2026*

BUDI95 is the government scheme that lets eligible Malaysians buy RON95 petrol at RM1.99 a litre, up to a monthly limit. Anyone outside the scheme, or past their limit, pays the full price the Ministry of Finance (MOF) resets every week, RM3.72 a litre for Nov 19 to 25, 2026, down from RM3.87 the week before.

The full price is what a litre costs with no help from the government, often called the unsubsidised price. A subsidy is the part of the price the government pays for you.

## You Qualify If You're A Malaysian Aged 16 Or Above With A MyKad And A Valid Driving Licence.

BUDI95 started on Sep 30, 2025. There's nothing to sign up for. At the petrol station you verify yourself with your MyKad, at a reader on the pump or inside the shop, and then pay however you like: cash, card or e-wallet.

A few groups get special treatment. Full-time e-hailing drivers get a bigger allowance than everyone else, and registered boat owners can qualify without a driving licence.

## Each Person Gets 300 Subsidised Litres A Month.

Your 300 litres reset on the 1st of every month. Once you've used them up, every extra litre that month is charged at the full price.

That limit has already moved once. The government cut it to 200 litres from April 1, 2026, citing the conflict in the Middle East, then put it back to 300 litres from Sep 1, 2026. The BUDI95 price itself has stayed the same the whole time.

## The Government's Share Grew From 61 Sen To RM1.73 A Litre.

On every BUDI95 litre, the government covers the difference between the BUDI95 price and that week's full price. When the scheme started, the full price of RON95 was RM2.60, so the government's part was 61 sen a litre.

For Nov 19 to 25, 2026, the full price is RM3.72. That means the government now covers RM1.73 of every BUDI95 litre, or 47% of the full price, which is a little less than you pay yourself. A week earlier, its part was RM1.88, or about 49%.

When presenting Budget 2027, the government put fuel subsidies at about RM40 billion. On Oct 24, 2026, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim said the government would keep the BUDI95 price where it is. If oil prices keep rising, he said, the cost could reach RM42 billion to RM45 billion a year.

## The Weekly Full Price Reaches You Past Your Limit.

If you drive your own car and stay within 300 litres a month, the weekly price changes don't touch your tank.

Things change if you go past your limit, or if you fill up for a business that isn't covered by the scheme. Those litres are charged at whatever MOF set for that week, and that price has gone from RM2.60 when BUDI95 started to RM3.72 for Nov 19 to 25, 2026.

Diesel works the same way under a separate scheme, BUDI Diesel, where eligible diesel vehicle owners pay RM2.10 a litre.

## MOF Sets Prices Weekly, And Your Allowance Resets Monthly.

- Every week, usually on Wednesday evening: MOF announces the next week's full fuel prices, which apply from Thursday
- Around Wednesday, Nov 25, 2026: MOF's full prices for Nov 26 to Dec 2, 2026, on its usual weekly timing
- Tuesday, Dec 1, 2026: everyone's BUDI95 allowance resets to 300 litres for the month
