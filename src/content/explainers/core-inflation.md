*Updated Nov 6, 2026*

Core inflation measures how fast prices in Malaysia are rising once volatile fresh food and government-set prices are left out, so it shows the underlying trend in what you pay. In September 2026 it was 1.7%, a little under the headline figure of 1.8%, which leaves nothing out.

That trend still covers everyday bills like rent, streaming subscriptions and mobile phone plans, which Bank Negara Malaysia (BNM) names among the items inside core. BNM reports core next to the headline figure in its monthly reports and in its statements on the [Overnight Policy Rate (OPR)](/explainers/opr/), the main interest rate it sets.

## The Department Of Statistics Malaysia (DOSM) Works Out Both Figures From One Basket.

DOSM tracks prices every month with the [consumer price index (CPI)](/explainers/inflation/), a fixed basket of what households buy. For core, DOSM takes the same basket and leaves out two kinds of prices: volatile fresh food, and prices administered by the government. Alcoholic drinks and tobacco have no core figure at all, because DOSM says every price in that group is set by the government.

Both figures compare prices with the same month a year earlier, and both come out on the same day.

## Core Shows The Trend, While The Headline Shows Everything.

DOSM says core is usually taken as a better guide to the underlying, long-run trend in prices. In September 2026, the headline figure slowed to 1.8% from 1.9% in August, partly because electricity charges and vegetable prices came down, BNM said. Core stayed at 1.7%, as slower price rises for mobile phone plans were offset by faster ones in other core items.

In February 2026, BNM's governor said core was expected to stay broadly stable and close to its long-term average in 2026. He said that reflected an economy still growing, without people and businesses spending so much that prices get pushed up too fast.

## BNM Gave Both Averages When It Held The OPR On Nov 5, 2026.

On Nov 5, 2026, BNM kept the OPR at 2.75%. Its statement said headline and core inflation averaged 1.8% and 1.9% so far in 2026. BNM expects both to rise a little in 2027, pointing to high global commodity prices and bad weather, but to stay contained.

## Core Inflation Was 1.7% In September 2026.

The 1.7% was down from 2.3% in December 2025 and January 2026. Core's 2026 average so far, 1.9%, sits inside the 1.8% to 2.3% that BNM projected for the year in March 2026.
