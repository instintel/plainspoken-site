*Updated Nov 20, 2026*

E&E exports are the electrical and electronic goods that Malaysia sells to other countries, from semiconductors, which are chips, to other electronic products. In September 2026 they made up 51.2% of everything Malaysia exported, up from 44.3% across 2025.

The Department of Statistics Malaysia (DOSM) and the Ministry of Investment, Trade and Industry (MITI) use "E&E" as the short name for these goods. Exports feed into [GDP](/explainers/gdp/), or gross domestic product, the measure of how much the whole economy produces. So E&E sits behind how fast Malaysia's economy grows. It also shows where factory demand is coming from.

## E&E Is The Biggest Single Part Of Malaysia's [Exports](/explainers/malaysia-exports/).

In 2025, these goods made up RM711.61 billion of the RM1.607 trillion Malaysia exported, MITI's figures show. In the first nine months of 2026 alone, they came to RM766.4 billion.

For October 2026, DOSM said E&E exports were RM58.5 billion higher than a year earlier, the main driver of the rise in total exports. Total exports that month rose 61.1% to RM239.2 billion.

## One Group Of Goods Carries A Lot Of The Trade Surplus.

A trade surplus means Malaysia sold more abroad than it bought. In 2025, the trade minister told Parliament on Oct 12, 2026, E&E trade left a surplus of RM145 billion. Everything else together left RM12 billion.

He also said Malaysia needs to sell more of its other goods abroad, and buy more of what it needs from firms at home.

## A Lot Rides On Demand For Chips.

Hong Leong Investment Bank (HLIB) Research called the global upturn in chip sales the main growth engine for Malaysia's exports. It named a pending US decision on tariffs, which are taxes on imports, as a big risk to that growth.

On Oct 13, 2026, the trade minister said Malaysia had no sign the US would put tariffs on its chips. He said the one issue still open in its US trade talks was what the US calls excess capacity, making more goods than buyers want, and no date had been given for a decision. If overseas demand for chips cools, the export figure is where it would show.

## The Latest E&E Figures Are For September And October 2026.

- September 2026: E&E exports RM110.2 billion, or 51.2% of total exports, up 69.2% from a year earlier, MITI's highest monthly figure for these goods
- October 2026: E&E exports RM58.5 billion higher than a year earlier, DOSM said
