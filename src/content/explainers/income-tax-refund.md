*Updated Nov 4, 2026*

An income tax refund is the money the Inland Revenue Board of Malaysia (LHDN) pays back into your bank account when more tax came off your pay than you owed. So far in 2026, up to Oct 31, LHDN has paid RM20.271 billion of refunds, against RM22.45 billion in the whole of 2025, the most in five years.

Of this year's total, RM6.84 billion went to 3.4 million individual taxpayers, up from RM6.38 billion at the end of September. Finance Minister II Amir Hamzah Azizan gave the latest figures to Parliament on Nov 3, 2026.

## Tax Comes Off Your Pay Every Month, And Your Yearly Return Checks It.

If you're on a salary, part of your tax is taken out of your pay before it reaches you. That's PCB, short for potongan cukai bulanan, the monthly tax deduction, and it's basically your tax paid in instalments through the year.

After the year ends, you file your income tax return online, through e-Filing on MyTax, LHDN's tax website. Form BE is for people with no business income and form B is for people who run a business. The return works out the tax you actually owe for the year, and LHDN compares that with the PCB already taken. If more came off your pay than you owe, the extra is your refund. If less, you pay the balance.

So the refund only starts moving once you file, LHDN says. For the 2025 year of assessment, meaning income earned in 2025, form BE was due on Apr 30, 2026, with e-Filing allowed until May 15, 2026.

## LHDN Aims To Pay An E-Filing Refund Within 30 Working Days.

Under its taxpayer charter, LHDN promises to pay a refund within 30 working days of getting a complete and correct e-Filing return, and up to 90 working days for one sent by post or by hand.

Most refunds go by electronic transfer straight to the bank account you registered with LHDN. Some go through DuitNow, linked to your identity card number, and some by cheque in the post. LHDN says refunds get held up when your bank details are wrong, inactive or not in your name. They also wait when you still owe tax from an earlier year, because that old tax comes out of the refund first. You can check your refund status on MyTax.

## The Number Of Taxpayers Still Waiting For A Refund Has Almost Halved.

At the end of 2025, more than one million taxpayers had excess tax credits, tax paid above what they owed that was still waiting to come back. By Oct 31, 2026, that number was down by almost half, to about 550,000, Amir Hamzah said.

The government says it pays individuals and micro, small and medium enterprises (MSMEs) first, and older cases before newer ones. If a refund comes later than the Income Tax Act 1967 allows, LHDN adds compensation of 2% a year.

## The 2024 Refunds Are Meant To Be Cleared By The End Of 2026.

- **Budget 2027:** presented in Parliament on Oct 9, 2026, it plans to raise the individual tax relief from RM9,000 to RM12,000 and cut the tax rate by one percentage point on taxable income between RM70,000 and RM150,000
- **The return for 2026 income:** LHDN plans a pilot that fills in some tax relief details for you from e-invoices, the digital receipts you can ask shops for, it said on May 9, 2026
- **By the end of 2026:** the Ministry of Finance (MOF) aims to finish paying refunds for the 2024 year of assessment, it said in January 2026
