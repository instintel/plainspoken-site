*Updated Nov 25, 2026*

Inflation is how fast prices are rising, compared with the same month a year earlier, so it's what your pay has to keep up with to buy as much as before. Malaysia's inflation was 1.9% in October 2026, up from 1.8% in September. That means the same mix of everyday spending cost 1.9% more than it did in October 2025.

It's measured every month by the Department of Statistics Malaysia (DOSM) with the consumer price index, or CPI.

## DOSM Prices The Same Basket Of Spending Every Month.

The CPI tracks what it costs to buy a fixed basket of goods and services. The amounts and the quality stay the same, so only price changes show up. The basket covers 13 main groups, from food and transport to housing, health and restaurants. The groups are weighted, so the ones people spend more on count for more. Food and drinks are the biggest, at 29.8% of the basket.

DOSM set the index at 100 in 2010. In October 2026 it stood at 137.7, against 135.1 a year earlier. That gap is the 1.9%.

DOSM also gives the change from the month before. Between September and October 2026, prices rose 0.1%. When inflation slows, prices are still rising, just more slowly. Only a figure below zero means prices actually fell.

## The Headline Is An Average, So Your Own Inflation Can Differ.

In October 2026, transport rose 4.2%, up from 2.5% in September, and DOSM said the transport group drove October's pick-up. Housing, water, electricity and gas rose 1.9%, up from 1.4%, while food and drinks rose 1.7%, down from 1.8%. So the price rise you feel depends on what you spend most on.

## Your Savings Have To Keep Up With Inflation Too.

Savings work the same way as pay: money earning less than the inflation rate slowly loses what it can buy.

Bank Negara Malaysia (BNM) also watches inflation when it sets the [Overnight Policy Rate (OPR)](/explainers/opr/), the rate behind floating-rate loans. BNM kept the OPR at 2.75% on Nov 5, 2026, and said it expects inflation to edge higher in 2027 while staying contained.

## Malaysia's Inflation Was 1.9% In October 2026.

BNM expects inflation to average 1.5% to 2.5% in 2026, and said on Nov 18, 2026 that it may rise gradually towards the year-end. The Ministry of Finance (MOF) expects inflation of 1.8% to 2.8% in 2027. Both MOF and BNM expect inflation to run higher in the early months of 2027.
