*Updated Nov 3, 2026*

The JS-SEZ is the Johor-Singapore Special Economic Zone, a part of Johor where Malaysia and Singapore work together to bring in new businesses and the jobs that come with them. From January 2025 to June 2026, companies got the go-ahead to invest RM88.83 billion there, and RM40.3 billion of that, or 45%, has been implemented. Implemented means the projects have begun on the ground.

Those implemented projects are expected to create 12,796 jobs, out of the 28,755 jobs the approved projects could create, Economy Minister Akmal Nasrullah Mohd Nasir said on Nov 2, 2026.

## Malaysia And Singapore Set Up The Zone Together In January 2025.

Both governments signed the agreement behind the zone on Jan 7, 2025, at the Malaysia-Singapore Leaders' Retreat, their leaders' yearly meeting. The zone covers more than 3,500 square kilometres, over four times the size of Singapore, across nine flagship areas, from Johor Bahru and Iskandar Puteri to Forest City and Desaru.

On the Malaysian side, companies go through one office, the Invest Malaysia Facilitation Centre Johor, which opened in February 2025 in Forest City. The Malaysian Investment Development Authority (MIDA), the federal agency that brings investment into Malaysia, runs it with two other agencies, so investors don't have to visit many offices for their approvals.

## The Zone Offers Lower Taxes To Companies And Skilled Staff.

Since Jan 1, 2025, companies making new investments in chosen industries, such as artificial intelligence (AI) and medical devices, can pay 5% tax on their profit for up to 15 years. Eligible knowledge workers employed in the zone, something like skilled professionals who meet the scheme's rules, can pay 15% income tax for 10 years. The Ministry of Finance (MOF) announced both in January 2025.

## Most Of The Approved Investment Came In 2025.

Of the RM88.83 billion approved up to June 2026, RM76.98 billion was approved in 2025 alone. The newest count came out in Singapore on Nov 2, 2026, at the first meeting of a new committee of ministers from both countries.

## A New Train Link And The Zone's Master Plan Come Next.

Running between Bukit Chagar in Johor Bahru and Woodlands North in Singapore, the Rapid Transit System (RTS) Link train takes about five minutes. It can carry up to 10,000 passengers an hour each way, its operator, RTS Operations, says. It was meant to open in January 2027. On Oct 2, 2026, the operator said passenger service is expected to start in February 2027 instead.

Meanwhile, the zone's master plan, its detailed plan for the coming years, was first due by the end of 2025 and then by Mar 30, 2026. Malaysia and Singapore now say it will be launched in December 2026.

- **Friday, Dec 4 and Saturday, Dec 5, 2026:** the 13th Malaysia-Singapore Leaders' Retreat, where Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim and Singapore's Prime Minister Lawrence Wong are due to launch the master plan
- **February 2027:** RTS Link passenger service expected to start
- **By March 2027:** a joint investment forum from both countries
