*Updated Nov 3, 2026*

The FBM KLCI is Malaysia's main share index: one number that follows the share prices of the biggest companies on Bursa Malaysia, the country's stock exchange. It tracks 30 companies today and grows to 50 from Dec 21, 2026. If you own Malaysian shares, or a unit trust that holds them, the KLCI is a quick read on how the biggest of them did that day.

Its full name is the FTSE Bursa Malaysia KLCI. Moves are given in points and in percent, and the percent is the one to look at, because it lets you compare days. On Nov 2, 2026, the KLCI rose 3.91 points, or 0.24%, to end at 1,619.51.

## The Companies Are Picked By Size, Twice A Year.

Under FTSE Russell's rules for the FTSE Bursa Malaysia index series, the KLCI holds the largest companies on Bursa Malaysia's Main Market by market value. That's a company's share price times its number of shares. The list is reviewed every June and December, so a company that grows can move in and one that shrinks can drop out.

## Bigger Companies Move The Index More.

Each company counts in proportion to its size, but only its free float counts: the shares that are available for anyone to buy and sell, leaving out large blocks held by long-term owners. A company with 15% or less of its shares free to trade can't get in at all. So a 5% jump in one of the largest companies moves the KLCI far more than a 5% jump in one of the smallest.

## The KLCI Doesn't Always Move With Your Shares Or The Economy.

Your own shares can go the other way, since the KLCI doesn't cover every company. It doesn't always follow the economy day to day either. On Oct 16, 2026, the KLCI fell nearly 1% on the same day Malaysia's growth figure came in better than expected.

Over a whole year it swings too. The KLCI ended October 2025 at 1,609.15, climbed to 1,740.88 at the end of January 2026, then ended October 2026 at 1,615.60, a little above where it started.

## The KLCI Grows From 30 To 50 Companies From Dec 21, 2026.

Bursa Malaysia and FTSE Russell announced the change on Aug 20, 2026, and it happens in two steps. On Dec 21, 2026, 20 more companies join, each counting at half its full weight. On June 21, 2027, they count in full. Spreading it out is meant to ease a rush of buying and selling on one day. FTSE Russell says the results of its December 2026 review come out on Dec 3, 2026.

Based on June 2026 figures, the bigger index would cover about 70% of the Main Market's total value, up from about 60%. It could also take in technology and energy companies, and REITs (companies that own buildings and pay out the rent they collect), for the first time.

## The Next Dates Are Dec 3 And Dec 21, 2026, And June 21, 2027.

- Thursday, Dec 3, 2026: FTSE Russell announces the results of its December review
- Monday, Dec 21, 2026: the KLCI grows to 50 companies, with the 20 new ones at half weight
- Monday, June 21, 2027: the 20 new companies count at full weight
