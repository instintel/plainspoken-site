*Updated Nov 21, 2026*

Malaysia's exports figure is the total value of the goods the country sells abroad in a month, the money overseas buyers pay Malaysian firms. In October 2026 it was RM239.2 billion, up 61.1% from RM148.4 billion a year earlier.

A bigger figure means more money coming in from abroad. The Department of Statistics Malaysia (DOSM) counts it in ringgit.

## DOSM Publishes Exports And Imports Once A Month, In The Middle Of The Next Month.

October's figure came out on Nov 20, 2026, and November's is due on Dec 18. Each release also gives imports, which are the goods Malaysia buys from abroad.

Exports minus imports gives the trade surplus, and a surplus means Malaysia sold more abroad than it bought. October 2026 was the 78th monthly surplus in a row, since May 2020, at RM41.8 billion. Imports that month were RM197.4 billion. The Ministry of Investment, Trade and Industry (MITI) counts 2025 as the 28th year in a row with a yearly surplus.

Exports are made up of two kinds of goods: those made in Malaysia, and re-exports, which are goods that came in from abroad and went out again.

## Electrical And Electronic Goods Are The Biggest Part.

They are called [E&E products](/explainers/electronics-exports/). In 2025 they made up 44.3% of Malaysia's exports, or RM711.61 billion, according to MITI. In October 2026 the share was higher, at 52.5%, the Malaysia External Trade Development Corporation (MATRADE) said. E&E exports were RM58.5 billion higher than a year earlier, more than any other kind of goods DOSM listed.

## Exports Feed Into Malaysia's Growth Rate.

The figure feeds into growth in [GDP](/explainers/gdp/), or gross domestic product, the measure of how much the whole economy produces. From July to September 2026, the economy grew 5.5%, DOSM said.

The figure is in ringgit, so it includes price changes as well as the amount shipped. In September 2026, DOSM's trade price measure showed export prices 8.1% higher than a year earlier, while the amount shipped was 42.9% higher.

## The Latest Figure Is RM239.2 Billion, For October 2026.

- October 2026 exports were RM239.2 billion, up 10.9% from September's RM215.8 billion. MITI had called September a record month.
- Exports plus imports came to RM436.6 billion, the first month above RM400 billion.
- Shipments to Taiwan rose the most from a year earlier, up RM23.7 billion, followed by the US, up RM19.9 billion.
- From January to October 2026, exports were 37.1% higher than a year earlier. The Ministry of Finance (MOF) projects 31% growth for the whole of 2026.
- The trade surplus for those ten months was RM274.8 billion, more than double a year earlier.
