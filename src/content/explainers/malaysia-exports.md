*Updated Nov 3, 2026*

Malaysia's exports figure is the total value of the goods the country sells abroad in a month, the money overseas buyers pay Malaysian firms. In September 2026 it was RM215.1 billion, up 54.5% from RM139.3 billion a year earlier.

A bigger figure means more money coming in from abroad. The Department of Statistics Malaysia (DOSM) counts it in ringgit.

## DOSM Publishes Exports And Imports Once A Month, In The Middle Of The Next Month.

August's figure came out on Sep 18, 2026, and September's on Oct 19. Each release also gives imports, which are the goods Malaysia buys from abroad.

Exports minus imports gives the trade surplus, and a surplus means Malaysia sold more abroad than it bought. September 2026 was the 77th monthly surplus in a row, since May 2020, at RM33.7 billion. Imports that month were RM181.4 billion. The Ministry of Investment, Trade and Industry (MITI) counts 2025 as the 28th year in a row with a yearly surplus.

Exports are made up of two kinds of goods: those made in Malaysia, and re-exports, which are goods that came in from abroad and went out again.

## Electrical And Electronic Goods Are The Biggest Part.

They are called [E&E products](/explainers/electronics-exports/). In 2025 they made up 44.3% of Malaysia's exports, or RM711.61 billion, according to MITI. In August 2026 the share was higher, at 48.4%. DOSM said that month's growth came mainly from higher E&E shipments.

## Exports Feed Into Malaysia's Growth Rate.

The figure feeds into growth in [GDP](/explainers/gdp/), or gross domestic product, the measure of how much the whole economy produces. DOSM's early estimate put growth for July to September 2026 at 5.3%.

The figure is in ringgit, so it includes price changes as well as the amount shipped. In August 2026, DOSM's trade price measure showed export prices 7.9% higher than a year earlier, while the amount shipped was 34.8% higher.

## The Latest Figure Is RM215.1 Billion, For September 2026.

- September 2026 exports were RM215.1 billion, up 12.6% from August's RM191.0 billion. It's the first month above RM200 billion.
- Shipments to the US led the rise from a year earlier, up 89.9% to RM38.03 billion.
- From January to September 2026, exports were RM1.57 trillion, up 34%. The Ministry of Finance (MOF) projects 31% growth for the whole of 2026.
- The trade surplus for those nine months was RM232.4 billion, more than double a year earlier.
