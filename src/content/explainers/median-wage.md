*Updated Oct 28, 2026*

The median wage is the pay of the worker in the exact middle: line everyone up from lowest to highest pay, and half earn more than the median while half earn less. Malaysia's median monthly wage for citizens in formal jobs was RM3,000 in June 2026.

## The median is not the average.

An average adds up everyone's pay and divides it by the number of workers, so a few very high earners can pull it up. The median doesn't move when a few people at the top earn more, which is why it's closer to what a typical worker takes home.

## DOSM measures it every quarter, from EPF and SOCSO records.

The Department of Statistics Malaysia (DOSM) publishes the figure in its quarterly Employee Wages Statistics (Formal Sector) report. The numbers come from contribution records at the Employees Provident Fund (EPF) and the Social Security Organisation (SOCSO), so they cover Malaysian citizens with formal jobs that pay into those schemes. Pay includes bonuses, commissions and allowances. People who work for themselves and unpaid family workers are not counted.

## Your pay against RM3,000 is only part of the picture.

Where you work matters. In June 2026 the median was RM4,245 in Kuala Lumpur and RM1,827 in Kelantan. Across the country, the bottom 10% of formal workers earned RM1,700 or less, and the top 10% earned at least RM9,600. Women's median was RM2,964 and men's was RM3,000.

A pay rise also means more when you set it against prices. The median rose 4.8% over the year to June 2026, while [consumer prices](/explainers/inflation/) rose 1.9% over the same year.

## The median has risen every year since 2023.

In June 2026 it was RM3,000, up RM136 from RM2,864 in June 2025. It was RM2,745 in June 2024 and RM2,600 in June 2023. The share of formal workers earning below RM1,700 fell to 8.2% in June 2026, from 10.4% a year earlier. DOSM counted 7.20 million citizens in formal jobs, up 3.3%. DOSM reports a median for each month, and this page compares June with June so the same month is set side by side.

## The next figures come in DOSM's third-quarter report.

DOSM's next quarterly Employee Wages Statistics report will cover July to September 2026. Until then, RM3,000 for June 2026 is the latest median, and the next report will show whether pay kept rising faster than prices.
