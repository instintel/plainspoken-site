*Updated Nov 3, 2026*

Malaysia's minimum wage is the lowest monthly pay an employer can legally offer a worker. Budget 2027 raises it from RM1,700 a month today to RM2,000 from June 2027, a rise of RM300 a month.

## The Government Sets The Minimum Wage, And Employers Must Follow It.

Each rate comes from a Minimum Wages Order. Not paying it is an offence under the National Wages Consultative Council Act 2011.

The last rise was on Feb 1, 2025, from RM1,500, the rate since May 1, 2022, to RM1,700. It applied first to employers with five or more employees, and to professional services regardless of size. Employers with fewer than five employees had until Aug 1, 2025.

## The Prime Minister Says The Rise Covers More Than Four Million Workers.

Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim announced the rise when he presented Budget 2027 on Oct 9, 2026. He said more than four million workers would be covered. Employers have until June 1, 2027 to get ready.

Budget 2027 also proposed a starting salary of RM2,500 a month for semi-skilled jobs and graduates. The Human Resources Ministry says it aims to publish starting-salary guidelines, set by job type and qualification, with the new order in June 2027. It is a different measure from the minimum wage.

## Some Smaller Businesses Are Exempt For Now.

Micro, small and medium-sized businesses, called MSMEs, with annual sales below RM50 million are excused from applying the new rate for now, to give them time to adjust how they run. On Oct 29, 2026, Human Resources Minister R. Ramanan told Parliament the government will bring the rise in for this group in phases. The Malaysian Employers Federation (MEF) welcomed the exemption. It also asked for clear rules on who qualifies and how the RM50 million sales figure will be assessed.

## Your Pay Floor Rises In June 2027 If Your Employer Is Covered.

If you earn RM1,700 and your employer is covered, your pay floor goes up by RM300 a month from June 2027. If you earn more than that, the rise doesn't change your pay directly.

If you hire people, the date to mark is June 1, 2027. Check whether your annual sales are under RM50 million, because that decides whether you can use the exemption for now. A pay rise only helps if it keeps up with prices, and [inflation](/explainers/inflation/) is the measure of how fast they are rising.

## The RM2,000 Rate Starts In June 2027.

- Minimum wage now: RM1,700 a month, since Feb 1, 2025
- Exemption for now: MSMEs with annual sales below RM50 million, with the rise to come to them in phases
- Not set yet: the rules on who qualifies for the exemption, and the dates of the phases
- Tuesday, June 1, 2027: the date employers have to be ready by, with the RM2,000 rate starting that month for employers outside the exemption
