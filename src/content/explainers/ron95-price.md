*Updated Nov 23, 2026*

The RON95 price most people mean is the full price of a litre of RON95 petrol, which the Ministry of Finance (MOF) resets every week. For Nov 19 to 25, 2026, it's RM3.72 a litre, up from RM2.65 a year earlier. If you're on [BUDI95](/explainers/budi95/), you pay a lower fixed price instead, up to your monthly limit.

You'll also see it called the unsubsidised price, meaning what a litre costs with no help from the government.

## MOF Sets Fuel Prices Once A Week, Using A Formula.

That formula is called the Automatic Pricing Mechanism (APM). Malaysia has set its pump prices weekly with it since March 2017. MOF usually announces the new prices on Wednesday evening, and they apply from Thursday for the next seven days.

Each weekly statement covers more than RON95:

- RON97 petrol
- RON95 at the full price, and RON95 under BUDI95
- Diesel at the full price
- Diesel under BUDI Diesel

## The Formula Follows What Fuel Cost The Week Before.

MOF says the APM takes into account the average price of the previous week. So the full price tracks what fuel was costing in the days before, which is why it can go up one week and come down the next. For Nov 19 to 25, 2026, MOF said the price of refined fuel in the region fell during the APM's calculation period. Refined fuel is the petrol and diesel made from crude oil. MOF said that fall was in line with lower world crude oil prices, the same link it gave for Nov 12 to 18. For Nov 5 to 11, MOF pointed to a drop in [Brent crude](/explainers/brent-oil-price/), the oil price in the news, near the end of the APM's calculation period.

Not every price moves, though. The government holds some of them fixed: RON95 under BUDI95 keeps its fixed price and BUDI Diesel stays at RM2.10, whatever the formula says that week.

## The Weekly Price Reaches You Once You Pay The Full Price.

If you're on BUDI95 and stay within your 300 litres a month, the weekly change doesn't reach your own tank. It does reach you once you go past your limit, or if you fill up RON97.

It also changes what the government spends. Because the BUDI95 price is fixed, every rise in the full price adds to the government's share of each BUDI95 litre, and every cut takes some off. For Nov 19 to 25, 2026, that share is RM1.73 a litre, about 47% of the full price, down from RM1.88 the week before.

## The Latest Prices Are For Nov 19 To 25, 2026.

- RON95, full price: RM3.72 a litre, down 15 sen from the week before
- RON97: RM4.00 a litre, down 20 sen
- Diesel, full price: RM4.72 a litre, down 15 sen

That's the third cut in a row, after 40 sen off for Nov 12 to 18 and 15 sen off for Nov 5 to 11. The full RON95 price is now the lowest since Aug 13 to 19, when it was RM3.62, and well below its peak of RM4.67 on Oct 8.

For Nov 20 to 26, 2025, a year earlier, a litre of RON95 at the full price cost RM2.65. Filling 40 litres at the full price cost about RM106 then, and costs about RM149 for Nov 19 to 25, 2026.
