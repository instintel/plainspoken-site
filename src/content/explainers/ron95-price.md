*Updated Nov 3, 2026*

The RON95 price most people mean is the full price of a litre of RON95 petrol, which the Ministry of Finance (MOF) resets every week. For Oct 29 to Nov 4, 2026, it's RM4.42 a litre, up from RM2.60 a year earlier. If you're on BUDI95, you pay a lower fixed price instead, up to your monthly limit.

You'll also see it called the unsubsidised price, meaning what a litre costs with no help from the government.

## MOF Sets Fuel Prices Once A Week, Using A Formula.

That formula is called the Automatic Pricing Mechanism (APM). Malaysia has set its pump prices weekly with it since April 2017. MOF usually announces the new prices on Wednesday evening, and they apply from Thursday for the next seven days.

Each weekly statement covers more than RON95:

- RON97 petrol
- RON95 at the full price, and RON95 under BUDI95
- Diesel in Peninsular Malaysia
- Diesel under BUDI Diesel

## The Formula Follows What Fuel Cost The Week Before.

MOF says the APM takes into account the average price of the previous week. So the full price tracks what fuel was costing in the days before, which is why it can go up one week and come down the next. For Oct 29 to Nov 4, 2026, MOF said [Brent crude](/explainers/brent-oil-price/), the oil price in the news, rose the week before on concerns that supply from West Asia could be disrupted.

Not every price moves, though. The government holds some of them fixed: RON95 under BUDI95 keeps its fixed price and BUDI Diesel stays at RM2.10, whatever the formula says that week.

## The Weekly Price Reaches You Once You Pay The Full Price.

If you're on BUDI95 and stay within your 300 litres a month, the weekly change doesn't reach your own tank. It does reach you once you go past your limit, if you fill up RON97, or if you buy fuel for a business that pays the full price. Every weekly move then shows up in your running costs.

It also changes what the government spends. Because the BUDI95 price is fixed, every rise in the full price adds to the government's share of each BUDI95 litre. For Oct 29 to Nov 4, 2026, that share is RM2.43 a litre, up from RM2.33 the week before.

## The Latest Prices Are For Oct 29 To Nov 4, 2026.

- RON95, full price: RM4.42 a litre, up 10 sen from the week before
- RON97: RM4.90 a litre, up 10 sen
- Diesel in Peninsular Malaysia: RM5.27 a litre, up 20 sen

That rise comes a week after a 30 sen cut. The full RON95 price is still below its peak of RM4.67 on Oct 8, and well above the RM3.77 of Sep 3.

For Oct 30 to Nov 5, 2025, a year earlier, a litre of RON95 at the full price cost RM2.60. Filling 40 litres at the full price cost about RM104 then, and costs about RM177 this week.
