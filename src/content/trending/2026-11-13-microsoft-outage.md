*1-minute read*

A global Microsoft outage that hit Outlook and Teams from early Friday, Malaysia time, reached at least six Malaysian banks, which reported email disruptions.

![Photo of a laptop with a blank screen on a desk in an empty office.](/images/trending/2026-11-13/photo-microsoft-outage-2026-11-13.jpg)

For Maybank customers, the money side kept running. The bank said its digital banking and ATMs were still working, and that only emails might be slow. RHB said email replies may be delayed, and CIMB pointed customers to its hotlines instead. AmBank, Bank Muamalat and Affin Bank reported similar issues.

Microsoft said a process on its servers that sets up secure connections became overwhelmed, so the servers stopped responding. It saw no sign of a cyberattack. SharePoint and its Copilot AI assistant were also hit, and the outage-tracking site Downdetector logged failures in 74 countries and territories.

In Malaysia, the National Cyber Security Agency (NACSA) said the cause was more likely technical than a cyberattack. It told government agencies and operators of critical systems to check their services and report any disruption.

By Friday morning, Microsoft said some of its servers were back and stable so far, and that recovery would be gradual.
