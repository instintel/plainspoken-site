*1-minute read*

Four investment funds run by AmanahRaya Investment Management (ARIM) now value the Zetrix AI sukuk they hold at zero, ARIM told investors in a memo reported by The Edge.

![Photo of three office ring binders, cream, orange and green, standing side by side.](/images/trending/2026-11-14/photo-zetrix-sukuk-2026-11-14.jpg)

A sukuk is the Islamic version of a bond: investors put money in, get paid profit along the way, then get their money back at the end. If you have money in one of the four funds, you can't take it out or add more for now. Dealings are suspended until Nov 23, and the zero covers only the Zetrix sukuk, not everything else the funds own.

The zero followed a missed payment. Zetrix AI, the Bursa-listed owner of MyEG Sdn Bhd, didn't pay RM25.68 million due on Nov 6, and MARC Ratings cut its RM2 billion sukuk programme to a default rating. On Friday the company told Bursa Malaysia it is currently unable to repay the full RM2 billion. Talks with sukukholders on changing the sukuk's rules go on, with no date set for their next meeting.

Across all the money ARIM manages, RM527.25 million sat in these sukuk at the end of September, about a tenth, RAM Ratings said.

ARIM says the zero doesn't mean giving up. It will keep trying to recover the money, and anything it gets back goes into the funds' value when it arrives. Recovery isn't assured, it said.
