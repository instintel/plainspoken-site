Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA 6) came out in Malaysia at midnight, and the Standard Edition costs RM339 on the PlayStation Store here. The Ultimate Edition is RM419, and the game comes 13 years after GTA V.

![Photo of a black game controller resting on a dark leather surface.](/images/trending/2026-11-19/photo-gta-6-2026-11-19.jpg)

GTA 6 runs on the PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series consoles, not the older PS4. No PC version has been announced. The cheapest PS5, the Digital Edition, is RM2,499 on Sony's Malaysian price list.

That's RM430 more than before Sony raised prices this year, citing inflation and higher production and distribution costs.

The PS5 with a disc drive went up RM300, to RM2,799. At the cheapest, console and game together come to RM2,838.

Even the physical copy here holds a download code, not a disc.

Worldwide, research firm Sensor Tower estimated the game had already sold about 17 million copies before launch, worth more than US$1.65 billion. About three-quarters of those pre-order sales were on the PS5, Yahoo Finance reported.
