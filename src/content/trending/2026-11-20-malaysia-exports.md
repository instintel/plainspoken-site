*2-minute read*

Malaysia's [exports](/explainers/malaysia-exports/) jumped 61.1% in October from a year earlier, and the country's trade topped RM400 billion in a single month for the first time.

![A large ship stacked high with containers sails out across calm water at sunset, with a low coastline far in the distance.](/images/trending/2026-11-20/photo-malaysia-exports-2026-11-20.jpg)

Exports are what Malaysia sells to other countries, and they came to RM239.2 billion, the Department of Statistics Malaysia (DOSM) said on Friday. Stronger exports, led by tech products, helped the economy grow 5.5% from July to September, Bank Negara Malaysia (BNM) said on Wednesday.

Add what Malaysia bought from abroad, and total trade was RM436.6 billion, up from RM276.3 billion in October last year.

[Electrical and electronic (E&E) products](/explainers/electronics-exports/) led October's rise in exports. Their exports rose by RM58.5 billion, more than any other kind of goods. By country, exports rose most to Taiwan, up RM23.7 billion, then the US, up RM19.9 billion.

Exports were also 10.9% higher than in September, a month the Ministry of Investment, Trade and Industry (MITI) had already called a record.

Malaysia bought more too. Imports rose 54.4% to RM197.4 billion. But it still sold RM41.8 billion more than it bought, more than double the gap a year ago. That's the 78th month in a row Malaysia has sold more than it bought, going back to May 2020.

From January to October, exports were 37.1% higher than in the same months of 2025.
