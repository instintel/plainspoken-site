Employers were fined RM56,900 in total in 46 prosecutions for breaking the RM1,700 [minimum wage](/explainers/minimum-wage/) rule, up to Aug 31, Parliament was told on Monday.

![Gloved hands guide an angle grinder across metal bars in a workshop, throwing off a spray of orange sparks.](/images/trending/2026-11-23/photo-minimum-wage-2026-11-23.jpg)

The minimum wage is the least any private employer, whatever its size, must pay you: RM1,700 a month under the Minimum Wages Order 2024. A manufacturing firm was fined RM8,000 and a services company RM24,000 for paying below it, Deputy Human Resources Minister Khairul Firdaus Akbar Khan said.

Labour inspectors also found 204 employers breaking the rule in 28,259 checks, and told them to fix it.

There were complaints too. The Labour Department received 264 about the minimum wage, and 185 of them were found valid.

The rate goes up next year. From June 1, 2027, the minimum wage rises to RM2,000 a month under Budget 2027. Smaller businesses (micro, small and medium firms) with yearly sales below RM50 million are exempt from the higher rate, though the government is encouraging them to pay it, he said.

## What happens next

- **Tuesday, June 1, 2027:** the minimum wage rises to RM2,000 a month, except at micro, small and medium firms with yearly sales below RM50 million.
